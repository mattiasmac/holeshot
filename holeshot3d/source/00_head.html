How to race

1. Burnout. Hold BURNOUT to heat the tires. The bar turns green when they're ready; too long and they go greasy. Skip it and you launch on cold rubber.

2. Stage. Press STAGE and keep your thumb down. The car creeps into the pre-stage beam, then the stage beam. Both bulbs lit means you're in.

3. The tree. Keep holding LAUNCH: the engine sits on the two-step at your launch RPM. Once both cars are in, the autostart fires the tree a random moment later. Street cars run the sportsman tree: three ambers count down half a second apart. The pro cars run the pro tree: all three ambers flash at once, green four-tenths later. Let go on the last amber – letting go is the launch, and the car needs time to roll out of the beam, so releasing on green is late. Let go before the tree is done and you red-light. Never let go and you sit there with no time while the rival takes the win.

4. Shift. The tach has a green window for each gear. Tap SHIFT inside it for a perfect shift. Early shifts bog the engine; hitting the limiter costs time.

5. Wheelspin. If the tach flares and the car doesn't move, you're spinning. Hold PEDAL to lift off the gas for a moment and hook back up. Lower your launch RPM in the garage if it keeps happening.

Reading the slip. R/T is reaction time (.000 is perfect). 60' is the launch. The 660' speed and the trap speed at the stripe tell you how much power got to the track. A holeshot win is beating a quicker car by leaving first.

Points. Earn points for every pass. Winning, holeshots, great lights, perfect shifts and personal bests all pay. Spend them in the garage on cars, tires, engines and transmissions.
